Digital Debug & Input Test Board

A compact perfboard tool built for quick debugging of small digital circuits. It includes Schmitt-trigger hex inverters, debounced pushbuttons, toggle switches, indicator LEDs, and JST headers for clean I/O breakout. The board lets me probe, stimulate, and verify digital logic without repeatedly breadboarding test inputs or wiring loose switches.
