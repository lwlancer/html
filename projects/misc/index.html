Miscellaneous Projects

Smaller builds, utilities, tools, and experiments that don’t warrant a full page.

Digital Debug & Input Test Board

A compact perfboard tool built for quick debugging of small digital circuits. It includes Schmitt-trigger hex inverters, debounced pushbuttons, toggle switches, indicator LEDs, and JST headers for clean I/O breakout. The board lets me probe, stimulate, and verify digital logic without repeatedly breadboarding test inputs or wiring loose switches.

Cyanotype Photography & Blueprints

A small darkroom experiment series using the classic cyanotype process. I mixed and coated sensitizer on paper, contact-printed my own photographs and drawings under UV light, then developed and toned the prints to bring out deep blues and higher contrast. Along the way I produced a set of blueprint-style images that double as both documentation and art.

Laser Cutter Enclosure Cabinet

A purpose-built wooden cabinet to safely house the converted laser cutter. The enclosure isn’t my finest piece of carpentry, but it does what matters: it contains the beam and the smoke. The lid uses OD7+ laser safety glass for viewing, and the cabinet is built to be nearly airtight so it can support proper ventilation once I tie it into the HEPA scrubber next to the machine. Given no light escapes and the suction on the scrubber is incredibly strong, I’m very happy with it.

3D-Printed Jeweler’s Ball Vise

To support the silversmithing work, I built a jeweler’s ball vise using mostly 3D-printed parts filled with cement for mass and stability. The design is based on Chris Borge’s tutorial on YouTube, with several of his STL files remixed to fit my tools and preferred workholding layouts. The result is a heavy, smooth-rotating vise that gives me precise control for engraving, filing, and stone setting without paying commercial ball-vise prices.

Design inspired by and adapted from Chris Borge’s open designs; STL files were remixed to suit my own tooling and workflow.
